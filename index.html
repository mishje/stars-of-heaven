Stars Of Heaven

Stars of Heaven is a band that honestly makes a bit of everything. One song might be rock, the next one techno, and the one after that sounds like it was recorded in someone's basement at 3 AM.
The band consists of Andrew Tate, Greta Thunberg and Eric Cartman, which is probably the last combination anyone would expect to see on stage together. Somehow, though, it works. Their music goes from completely normal songs to stuff that makes absolutely no sense.
They don't really stick to one genre, and that's kind of the point.

Stars of Heaven started in Nakhchivan City, Azerbaijan, where Andrew Tate, Greta Thunberg and Eric Cartman met outside a kebab shop and recorded a song on an old karaoke machine. They uploaded it as a joke, but it somehow went viral.
Their first album, Stars of Heaven, was a huge success, followed by the even stranger Stars of Heaven II. Then came Gamla Norrvägen, which turned them into the biggest band in the world.
Nobody really knows how they got there, but somehow, it worked.
